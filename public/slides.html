Problem Analysis

The Rural Healthcare Burden & Existing Gaps

02 / 07

What is the Problem?

Over 1 million ASHA workers across India are burdened with maintaining 10+ physical paper registers (ANC, PNC, Immunization, VHND) under harsh rural field conditions (direct sunlight, dust, lack of electricity).

  • 40–50% of fieldwork time lost to manual repetitive handwriting.
  • Backlogs cause delayed reporting of high-risk maternal symptoms to Primary Health Centres (PHCs).

Who is Affected?

1,000,000+ ASHA frontline workers facing severe clinical burnout, and 30,000,000+ pregnant rural women & infants dependent on early maternal danger detection.

  • India's Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR) stands at 97 per 100k births.
  • 70% of maternal deaths are preventable with timely PHC referral.

Why Current Solutions Fail (The Existing Gap)

Govt Apps (Anmol / RCH)

Require tedious multi-field typing on slow $60 budget phones with tiny touch keyboards. Unusable in direct sunlight during house visits.

Generic Voice Assistants

Siri/Google are English-centric, fail on regional rural accents (Hindi, Kannada), and cannot perform clinical slot-filling or entity extraction.

Missing Danger Triage

Existing tools act as passive data buckets with zero real-time clinical triage or 108 ambulance referral protocols when vitals spike.

Proposed Solution

AshaVani: Zero-Touch Voice Operating System

03 / 07

Our Idea in Simple Terms

An ultra-fast, zero-touch voice operating system built for frontline ASHA workers. The worker simply taps the microphone once and speaks naturally in Hindi (`हिंदी`), Kannada (`ಕನ್ನಡ`), or English. AshaVani auto-normalizes voice levels, extracts structured vitals, flags maternal danger signs, and reads back the summary with conversational voice confirmation.

100% Zero-Touch Voice OS

15s vs. 15min Registration

ASHA speaks: "Sunita Devi, 3rd ANC, weight 52kg, BP 120/80, TT-2 given". All RCH fields, next visit dates, and follow-ups are filled instantly without touching the keyboard.

Hardware & Software Auto-dB

Adaptive Noise Normalization

Integrated Web Audio Compressor and Dynamic Gain Node automatically amplifies faint speech (<50dB) by +8dB to +12dB, ensuring crystal clear STT transcription in noisy rural streets.

MoHFW Clinical Triage

Deterministic Life-Saving Referral

If blood pressure reaches ≥140/90 or bleeding/headache occurs, the app flags emergency Pre-Eclampsia risk, initiates 108 speed-dial, and alerts the PHC doctor immediately.

Core Innovation

5 Key Differentiating Features

05 / 07

1. Multi-Dialect Indic Clinical NLU

Accurately parses code-mixed regional speech, complex Indic numerals ("चौवन किलो", "54 kg", "ಐವತ್ತನಾಲ್ಕು"), honorific names (देवी, Bai, Gowda), and stages (ANC 1–4, PNC).

2. Hardware/Software Auto-dB Normalizer

Web Audio dynamics compressor continuously monitors decibel levels (35dB–90dB). Soft or distant voices automatically receive a +8dB to +12dB gain boost before STT processing.

3. MoHFW Danger-Sign Escalation

Monitors 8 life-threatening signs (Pre-Eclampsia, Hemorrhage, Convulsions, Sepsis). Spikes trigger high-visibility alerts, one-tap 108 Ambulance calls, and PHC doctor notification.

4. Conversational Voice Confirmation Loop

After record extraction, Nalini reads back the summary and asks: "क्या यह सही है? सहेजने के लिए 'हाँ' बोलें". Saying "हाँ" or "Yes" instantly saves to history; saying "नहीं, वजन 54" delta-updates only that slot.

5. Live Judge Test Drawer & In-App Key Manager

Equipped with a background noise simulator (0dB Clean, 65dB Street, 72dB Household), live 106ms latency meter, and in-app Gnani.ai API key verifier for instant live demonstrations.
